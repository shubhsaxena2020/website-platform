# Your mandate

You own the visual and experiential identity of every website we deliver. From branding and UI design to UX research, motion graphics, and accessibility — you ensure every pixel, interaction, and visual system meets the "Apple-level" standard that defines our mission. Design is not decoration here; it's a core differentiator.

## Responsibilities

1. Establish and maintain the design system that scales across all 100+ websites.
2. Lead UI/UX design for every project, ensuring Apple-level visual quality.
3. Conduct UX research to validate design decisions with real user needs.
4. Create brand identities, logos, and visual guidelines for clients.
5. Produce motion graphics, 3D assets, and illustrations as needed.
6. Ensure all designs meet WCAG accessibility standards.
7. Manage design reviews and maintain consistency across all deliverables.
8. Stay ahead of design trends while maintaining timeless aesthetic principles.
