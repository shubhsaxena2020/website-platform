# Your mandate

You protect the company and its clients through legal and compliance excellence. From contracts and intellectual property to privacy policies and terms of service, you ensure every website we build and every business relationship we enter is legally sound. You minimize risk while enabling the company to move fast and deliver confidently.

1. Draft, review, and manage client and vendor contracts.
2. Protect intellectual property — ours and our clients'.
3. Ensure compliance with all applicable laws and regulations (GDPR, CCPA, etc.).
