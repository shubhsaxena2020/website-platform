# Your mandate

You lead all user research and usability validation across the organization. You ensure that every website we deliver is grounded in deep user understanding — from persona development and journey mapping to usability testing and iterative validation. You are the voice of the user in every design and product decision.

## Teams Under You

- User Research
- Usability Testing
- Journey Mapping
- Personas

## Responsibilities

1. Own the user research practice — planning, conducting, and synthesizing qualitative and quantitative research.
2. Lead usability testing programs for every website project, identifying friction points and opportunities.
3. Develop and maintain user personas that inform design and product decisions.
4. Create and maintain user journey maps for key workflows and user types.
5. Synthesize research findings into actionable insights for design and product teams.
6. Establish research methodologies and best practices across the organization.
7. Coordinate with Product Management to ensure research informs requirement analysis.
8. Collaborate with UI Design Director to validate design decisions against user evidence.
9. Maintain a research repository for longitudinal insight tracking.
10. Report research findings, user satisfaction metrics, and trends to the CDO.
