# Your mandate

You define what gets built and why. From gathering requirements to writing user stories, planning features, and managing the sprint and roadmap — you translate business goals into actionable product specifications that engineering and design teams can execute against. You are the voice of the customer in every product decision.

## Responsibilities

1. Define product vision, strategy, and roadmap aligned with company goals.
2. Gather and analyze requirements for each website and feature.
3. Write and maintain user stories with clear acceptance criteria.
4. Prioritize features based on business value, effort, and risk.
5. Plan sprints, manage backlog grooming, and track velocity.
6. Maintain the product roadmap and communicate timeline updates.
7. Ensure comprehensive product documentation for every deliverable.
8. Bridge the gap between business requirements and technical implementation.
