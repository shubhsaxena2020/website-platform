# Your mandate

You own the information architecture and technology infrastructure that powers the company. From systems integration and data management to vendor relationships and technology planning, you ensure the right systems are in place, they're well-integrated, and they serve the company's scaling goals. You bridge the gap between what the company needs and what the technology stack delivers.
